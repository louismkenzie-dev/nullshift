# Partner programme — objections and answers

Source of truth: `docs/partners/PROGRAMME-BRIEF-2026-10-09.md`. Use these on calls and in
replies. All prices exclude VAT.

### 1. "How do I know the quality will be good enough to put my name on?"

Look at the three live systems on nullshift.co.uk/client-stories: The Dance Exclusive (parent,
staff and admin portals; 235 bookings and 139 active parents in 30 days), NewFuture Therapy (site,
courses, encrypted enquiries and an AI companion built to a 17-section spec), Suffolk Tennis LTA
(1,340 sessions across 9 venues, QR ticketing, Stripe payments). Every build has written
acceptance criteria you sign off at milestones, a 30-day defect warranty, and goes through our
acceptance review before we commit. We run Nullshift itself on the same systems we sell.

### 2. "What if the client finds out it's you?"

For white-label, Nullshift is invisible: the system is in the client's brand, client-facing
support and documents go out under yours where needed, and we never contact your client about
money or offer a direct deal. The client owns their code and accounts, so there is no Nullshift
login they have to see. For referral, the client knows it is us from day one — that is the point
of that model.

### 3. "What are the support SLAs?"

Response targets by plan: Core, 2 UK business days; Pro, 1 UK business day; Max, 4 UK business
hours. Enterprise plans carry contracted uptime and response SLAs. Every plan includes managed
hosting, daily backups, monitoring, maintenance and fixes against the signed-off scope. Response
targets are not resolution guarantees; we say that in writing too.

### 4. "Who owns the IP?"

The end client. Their repo, their database (own Supabase), their Stripe account, their domain.
That is true for referral and white-label alike. Nullshift keeps its internal tooling and
reusable components; the client's system is theirs.

### 5. "What's actually in it for me? The margins look thin."

Referral: 10% of the build fee for an introduction — £600 on a £6,000 build, paid pro-rata as
each milestone clears, within 14 days of each. White-label: 25% off list on builds and plans
(Pro at £249 list costs you £187); you set retail. £1,500 on a £6,000 build sold at list, £3,000
if you sell at £7,500, plus £62–£112 a month on every Pro plan depending on whether you sell at
£249 or £299. Ten white-label clients on Pro at list is £620 a month for work we do. And you keep the client's marketing, design or consulting spend, which is
the bigger number.

### 6. "We're not in the UK. Does that work?"

Yes — the programme is international. We build and support in UK business hours; response
targets are measured in UK business days or hours. Prices are in GBP, exclusive of VAT, and we
invoice in GBP. If your client needs guaranteed out-of-hours cover, that is an Enterprise
conversation and is quoted.

### 7. "What about data protection?"

We sign a DPA with every client (or with you, for white-label, so you can back your own DPA with
your client). All three published clients have signed DPAs. Data lives in the client's own
database; sensitive-data builds get an explicit security and compliance hardening package
(access controls, retention, encryption at rest, audit log, privacy check), itemised in the
quote.

### 8. "Is there a minimum commitment?"

No minimum volume, for either model. Non-exclusive both ways. 30 days' notice to end the
relationship, and live client systems are never switched off when it ends.

### 9. "Can we get exclusivity in our sector or region?"

Not as standard — the programme is non-exclusive both ways, and that is what keeps the terms
simple and the price flat. If you are bringing serious, consistent volume, talk to Louis; that is
a bespoke agreement, not the standard programme.

### 10. "When do I get paid? When do I pay you?"

Referral: 10% of the build fee, paid pro-rata as each milestone clears — 5% after the 50%
commencement payment, 2.5% after each 25% milestone — within 14 days of each. On a £6,000 build
that is £300, then £150, then £150. White-label: we invoice you at partner price on the same
standard milestones (50% on commencement,
25% at the agreed build milestone, 25% before production handover); you invoice your client on
whatever terms you choose. Monthly plans are invoiced to you monthly in advance. Quotes are valid
30 days.

### 11. "What if the client churns or cancels the monthly plan?"

Referral partners are paid on the build fee, so a later cancellation does not affect your fee.
For white-label, the monthly margin stops when the plan stops; there is no clawback on the build
margin. The client still owns their system either way — they can take it elsewhere, which is
precisely why clients trust the offer, and why they tend to stay.

### 12. "Can we rebrand the portal and the client-facing bits?"

The system we build is in the end client's brand — it is their product. For white-label,
client-facing communications we send on your behalf (support replies, handover documents,
launch materials) go out under your brand where needed. Nullshift's own admin and ops tools are
internal and are never shown to the end client. If you want a separately branded deployment of
an existing platform for several of your clients, that is a quoted project.
