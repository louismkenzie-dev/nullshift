How a project runs

  1. Estimate — you (or we) put the client's size and feature list into the guided estimator;

it returns a build guide price with a range and the monthly plan options. Quote valid 30 days.

  1. Acceptance review — every client and every piece of work is reviewed before we commit.
  2. Build — fixed price, milestone billing (50% on commencement, 25% at the agreed build

milestone, 25% before production handover). Most builds live in 2–4 weeks.

  1. Launch — testing, go-live, training and handover notes. Client owns the code and accounts.
  2. Monthly plan — Core, Pro or Max: hosting, maintenance, monitoring, support. New features

are quoted separately as fixed-price projects.
