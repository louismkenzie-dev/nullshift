# Nullshift Partner Programme — source-of-truth brief (2026-10-09)

Decisions made by Louis McKenzie (founder) on 2026-10-09. Every page, deck, email, agreement
and admin feature about the partner programme must agree with this file.

## Two ways to work with us

1. **Referral partner** — the agency introduces a client; Nullshift sells, builds and runs the
   system under the Nullshift brand. The partner earns **10% of the build fee** (paid within
   14 days of the client's build payment clearing). No share of monthly. Non-exclusive.
2. **White-label partner** — the agency sells under its own brand. The partner buys Nullshift
   builds and monthly plans at **25% off list** (list = the Core / Pro / Max plan prices and the
   guided-estimator build fee), sets its own retail price, and keeps the difference. Nullshift
   is invisible to the end client. **The agency hands management and responsibility to
   Nullshift**: hosting, support, delivery, maintenance and the monthly plan are run by
   Nullshift (through the partner's brand where needed). Non-exclusive. No minimum volume.
   30 days' notice to end the relationship; live client systems are never switched off.

## Scope and acceptance

- All Nullshift services are in scope: bespoke websites, booking systems, client portals,
  CRMs, dashboards, automation, AI assistants/agents, e-commerce, course/membership platforms.
- No sector is refused outright, but **every client and piece of work goes through an
  acceptance review first** (Nullshift's existing acceptance gate). Say "subject to
  acceptance review" rather than listing banned sectors.

## Who we are targeting (for outreach, not for the public page)

- Growth consultants first; then marketing / SEO / PPC agencies, design and brand studios,
  social media managers, accountants/bookkeepers. International. 2–20 staff, no in-house
  dev team. ~60–75 new agencies contacted per week. No cold calling.
- Warm lead: The Sunday Commerce (Louis knows the owner; interest already expressed).

## Positioning rules

- Studio-led voice. Nullshift is a capable UK software studio with a growing client base;
  write as "we", never as a solo freelancer. Founder cameos (Louis) are fine.
- Proof: The Dance Exclusive, New Future Therapy, LTA Suffolk Tennis (see /client-stories;
  the facts there are the only public results we quote).
- **Do NOT use the "no monthly ransom" line** or attack monthly retainers — Nullshift sells a
  monthly plan. The ownership point can still be made: clients own their code and accounts.
- No revenue claims beyond the client stories. Do not call Nullshift an "AI agency".
- Prices: plans from £40 / £80 / £120 per month (Core / Pro / Max, scale-banded); builds are
  quoted via the guided estimator (apps/web/lib/commercial). Partner pricing = 25% off these.
