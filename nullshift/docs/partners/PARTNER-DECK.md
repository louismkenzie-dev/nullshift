## Slide 2 — The problem: your clients are asking for software

- "Can you build us a booking system / client portal / CRM / members' area?"
- You do not have a dev team, and hiring one for occasional projects does not add up
- Outsourcing to a freelancer is a gamble on quality, timescales and who maintains it afterwards
- Saying no sends the client (and the budget) to someone else — often someone who then wants
  the marketing work too

**Speaker notes.** Make this their story. Ask: "When was the last time a client asked you for
something you had to turn away?" Most agencies have one from the last quarter. The cost is not
the lost project; it is the relationship opening up to a competitor who does both.

## Slide 4 — Referral partner: the numbers

**You introduce the client. We do the rest. You earn 10% of the build fee.**

Worked example — a booking, payments and automated-messaging build:

| | |
|---|---|
| Build fee (list, from the guided estimator) | £6,000 |

| Paid | pro-rata as each 50 / 25 / 25 milestone clears: £300, £150, £150 — each within 14 days |

- No share of the monthly plan; no ongoing obligations
- The client remains yours for everything you already do for them

**Speaker notes.** £600 for an introduction. Three introductions a quarter at that size is
£1,800 for a few emails. Stress the timing — each slice is paid within 14 days of the matching
milestone clearing. It is a real, dated commitment, not "when we get round to it".

## Slide 5 — White-label partner: the numbers

**You sell it under your brand. You buy from us at 25% off list and keep the difference.**

Worked example — the same £6,000 build on the Pro plan:

| | List | You pay (25% off) | You sell at | Your margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build fee | £6,000 | £4,500 | £6,000 (list) | **£1,500** |
| Build fee, priced above list | £6,000 | £4,500 | £7,500 | **£3,000** |
| Pro plan, per month | £249 | £187 | £249 (list) | **£62 / month** |
| Pro plan, priced above list | £249 | £187 | £299 | **£112 / month** |

- Nullshift is invisible to the end client
- You set the retail price; we never contact your client about money
- We run hosting, support, maintenance and the monthly plan through your brand where needed

**Speaker notes.** The build margin is the headline; the monthly margin is the business. Ten
white-label clients on Pro sold at list is £620 a month of margin for work we do; at £299 it is
£1,120. Partner prices are list × 0.75 rounded up to the pound: £112 / £187 / £300 base. Say plainly: "You
own the relationship and the invoice. We own the delivery and the 3am problems."

## Slide 7 — How a project runs

1. **Estimate** — you (or we) put the client's size and feature list into the guided estimator;
   it returns a build guide price with a range and the monthly plan options. Quote valid 30 days.
2. **Acceptance review** — every client and every piece of work is reviewed before we commit.
3. **Build** — fixed price, milestone billing (50% on commencement, 25% at the agreed build
   milestone, 25% before production handover). Most builds live in 2–4 weeks.
4. **Launch** — testing, go-live, training and handover notes. Client owns the code and accounts.
5. **Monthly plan** — Core, Pro or Max: hosting, maintenance, monitoring, support. New features
   are quoted separately as fixed-price projects.

**Speaker notes.** The acceptance review is a feature, not friction: it is how we keep delivery
quality high enough to put our name — or theirs — on. For white-label, step 1 can happen on a
call with the partner in the room and the end client never sees Nullshift.

## Slide 10 — Terms in one slide

- **Referral:** 10% of the build fee, paid pro-rata as each 50 / 25 / 25 milestone clears, within
  14 days of each. No share of monthly. Non-exclusive.
- **White-label:** 25% off list on builds and monthly plans (rounded up to the pound). List = the
  Core / Pro / Max plan prices (from £149 / £249 / £399 a month) and the guided-estimator build
  fee. You set retail. Builds invoiced to you on the same milestones; plans monthly in advance.
  Non-exclusive. No minimum volume.
- **Delivery, hosting, support, maintenance and the monthly plan** are run by Nullshift.
- **Acceptance:** every client and every piece of work is subject to acceptance review.
- **Ending the relationship:** 30 days' notice, either side. Live client systems are never
  switched off.
- **Ownership:** the end client owns their code, database and accounts.
- **Scope:** international partners welcome; prices in GBP, exclusive of VAT.

**Speaker notes.** Read it out; do not paraphrase. If asked for a contract, say the partner
agreement mirrors this slide and the rate card and we will send it after the call.
