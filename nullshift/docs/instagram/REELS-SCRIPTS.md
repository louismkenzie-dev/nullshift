Numbering matches `media_brief` references in CALENDAR-30-DAYS.json. All paths are under
`apps/web/public/`. Format: 1080×1920, 9:16, captions burned in or Instagram auto-captions
switched on; brand colours `#0B0F0E` (ink) and `#10B981` (emerald); type: the site's display
sans in caps for hooks, mono for labels.

Hard rules (from `docs/partners/PROGRAMME-BRIEF-2026-10-09.md`): "we" voice; founder cameos
fine; never "no monthly ransom" or any dig at monthly fees; never "AI agency"; only the three
client stories' facts; public plan prices are Core £149 / Pro £249 / Max £399 per month.
Screen-records of `/start` use a **fictional test business** — never a real client's
details. `/systems-lab` redirects to `/start`; do not film it.

## Production notes

- Export at 1080×1920, H.264, ≤ 60 Mbps, 30 fps; under 90s keeps the Reels tab eligible.
- Keep the top 250px and bottom 400px clear of text (Instagram UI overlays).
- Cover frames: upload a custom cover (dark card + hook text) so the grid stays uniform.
- Every reel gets its own end card with the wordmark and one URL — spoken as a URL, since
  captions are not clickable.
- Generated/AI imagery: none of the 12 scripts need it. If a future reel does, mark it
  "NEEDS LOUIS APPROVAL (generated media)" in the calendar and keep it to abstract brand
  visuals — never client work.
