## Rules of engagement

- Send Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00–10:00 in the **recipient's** local time.
- Maximum 15 new first-touch emails per day from louis@nullshift.co.uk; aim for 60–75 new agencies
  a week across email, LinkedIn and Instagram.
- Stop the sequence on **any** reply, including out-of-office; a human picks it up.
- Honour opt-outs immediately, across every channel. Never re-add an opted-out contact.
- One personalised line per E1, written by a person. If you cannot write it, do not send.
- No cold calling.
- Every claim comes from nullshift.co.uk/client-stories or the rate card. Nothing else.
- Log every touch and reply in the CRM the same day.
